AAC tools

AAC stands for Augmentative and Alternative Communication. These are the tools we use and build so that everyone can be heard and understood.

Low-tech to high-tech

From recordable-button devices like the Pocket Go-Talk to AI apps that read sign language, we match the tool to the person, not the other way round.
